Inference is pretty well established as its own stream within the AI world right now.

At first glance it seems pretty well covered and open questions boil down
to the best ways to slice and dice model weights for serving.

Everything fits into neat little boxes - until it doesn't.

One of the comments on my original dev.to article questioned why models
kept flipping scores on different GPUs.

Same model file, same llama.cpp build, temperature zero, two GPUs.

Why do they give different answers?

A GTX 1650 Ti laptop answers one GSM8K question with $20. A Tesla T4 answers the same question with 2000.

Each card repeats itself exactly, on every token, every time. What differs is the number of processors on each chip: llama.cpp splits each big matrix sum into one share per processor, 16 on the laptop and 40 on the T4, and float32 rounds the last digits differently for each grouping.

Most of the time that changes nothing. At a near-tie between two words, " spent" or " saved", it picks the other word, and the rest of the answer follows.

At default settings the two cards word 165 of 250 answers differently. One stock setting, -ub 1, makes them match on every token, for about 15% more run time.

After about a week of runs, and analysis- I finally had my answer.

Rounding.
