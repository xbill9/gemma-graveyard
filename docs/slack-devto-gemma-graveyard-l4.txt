Same nine Gemma 4 E2B GGUF files and the same llama.cpp build, run on an NVIDIA L4 (g6.xlarge) and a Tesla T4, 250 GSM8K questions at every quantization rung from BF16 to Q2_K.
The L4 decodes 1.25 to 1.89 times faster at every rung, and Q5_K_M is the best trade on both cards: 228 of 250 right on the L4 at 2.15 times BF16's speed.
Switching GPUs moves grades too: at Q5_K_M the two cards grade 7 questions differently, against 8 for quantizing from BF16 to Q5_K_M.
