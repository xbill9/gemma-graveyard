Four GPUs, One Quantization Ladder: Gemma 4 E2B From a 4 GB Laptop to an NVIDIA L4

Gemma 4 E2B from BF16 to Q2\_K on a Tesla T4, an NVIDIA L4, a GTX 1650 laptop and an RTX 3050 desktop, with byte-identical GGUF files and the same llama.cpp build. The ladder keeps its shape on all four cards, 218 questions die on all four, and any two GPUs grade 2 to 4 questions differently at BF16.

What is in it:

- 🟢 All nine GGUFs and the QAT file match the T4's sha256 on every machine, and BF16 gives identical text twice on 50 questions on every card.
- 🟢 The ladder keeps its shape on all four GPUs: F16 to Q5\_K\_M lose 1 to 8, Q4\_K\_M and Q4\_0 lose 9 to 19, Q3\_K\_M loses 42 to 49.
- 🟢 Q5\_K\_M is the best trade on every card: 226 to 231 right at 1.21 to 1.35 times Q8\_0's speed.
- 🟢 A 4 GB GTX 1650 runs every quantized rung whole on the GPU; BF16 and F16 run split, 14 of 36 layers on the GPU, at 9.2 and 9.6 tokens per second.
