More Gemma 4 adventures.
Same model files, same llama.cpp build, two different GPUs. Do they agree on which answers a quantized model gets wrong?

Gemma 4 E2B, nine quantization rungs from BF16 down to Q2_K, byte-identical GGUF files, 250 GSM8K questions, on an NVIDIA L4 in AWS and a Tesla T4 in Google Cloud.

The L4 decodes 1.25 to 1.89 times faster than the T4 at every rung.

Q5_K_M is the best trade on both cards: 228 of 250 right on the L4 against BF16's 229, at 2.15 times BF16's speed.

Switching GPUs moves grades too. At Q5_K_M the two cards grade 7 questions differently, against 8 for quantizing from BF16 to Q5_K_M on the T4.
