More Gemma 4 adventures.
Same model files, same llama.cpp build, four NVIDIA GPUs from a 4 GB laptop to an L4. Does the quantization ladder keep its shape?

Gemma 4 E2B, nine quantization rungs from BF16 down to Q2_K, byte-identical GGUF files, on a Tesla T4, an NVIDIA L4, a GTX 1650 laptop and an RTX 3050 desktop.

The ladder keeps its shape on all four: F16 to Q5_K_M lose 1 to 8 questions, Q4_K_M and Q4_0 lose 9 to 19, Q3_K_M loses 42 to 49.

Q5_K_M is the best trade on every card: 226 to 231 right at 1.21 to 1.35 times Q8_0's speed.

A 4 GB GTX 1650 runs every quantized rung whole on the GPU. 218 questions die on all four cards, and any two GPUs grade 2 to 4 questions differently at BF16.
